# Parlora

**A local-first voice tutor for practising spoken languages.**
Talk out loud with an AI conversation partner that adapts to your CEFR level
(A1–C2) and shows corrections on screen — all running on your own machine.

> 🚧 **Work in progress.** Parlora is in its first phase: the project skeleton
> is being built. There is nothing to install yet. English is the first
> language; more languages are planned.

## Principles

- **Private by design** — speech recognition, the language model and speech
  synthesis run locally. Your voice and transcripts never leave your computer.
- **Speaking first** — natural, spoken conversation; corrections arrive in
  writing so they never interrupt the flow.
- **Built to grow** — multi-language from the start, designed to be embedded
  in a larger language-learning platform.
